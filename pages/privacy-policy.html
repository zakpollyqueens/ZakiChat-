Privacy Policy

This is ZakiChat's application Privacy Policy. It is separate from the Privacy Settings page.

Depending on the features you use, ZakiChat may process account information such as your name, username, email address, phone number, profile information, and application settings.

Information needed to provide chats, groups, updates, calls, account features, security features, and other requested services may be processed by the systems supporting ZakiChat.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide and maintain the service, authenticate accounts, deliver requested features, protect accounts and systems, and improve the application.

4. Privacy Controls

ZakiChat provides privacy and account controls that allow users to manage available visibility and communication preferences.

5. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Service Providers

ZakiChat may rely on third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate features such as authentication, databases, hosting, and communications.

7. Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, meet operational requirements, or comply with applicable obligations.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ZakiChat's features and data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version.
