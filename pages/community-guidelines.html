Community Guidelines

ZakiChat · Effective 20 September 2026
These guidelines help keep conversations respectful and the service safe.

Respect Others

Communicate respectfully. Do not engage in targeted harassment, intimidation, threats, or abusive behavior.

Identity and Impersonation

Do not impersonate another person, organization, or account in a misleading or harmful way.

Safety and Fraud

Do not use ZakiChat for scams, fraud, credential theft, unauthorized access, malware, or other harmful activity.

Privacy

Respect other people's personal information. Do not share private information without appropriate permission or use it to harm others.

Content and Enforcement

Content or accounts that violate these guidelines may be restricted or removed where appropriate. Enforcement may depend on the circumstances and available information.

Updates

These guidelines may change as ZakiChat develops and new safety requirements arise.
