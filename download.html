Installation steps

  1. Tap the download button and wait for the APK download to finish.

Safety tip: Download only from this official page or the linked Molan Chat GitHub releases. Choose the app-release.apk production build, not app-debug.apk. Android may display an outside-Play-Store warning for APK installations.
